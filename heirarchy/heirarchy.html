I really liked this assignment. It is my first official time attempting any kind of coding work. Being a creative writing minor, any literary related project is always exciting to me. I have always wanted to go deeper in my own design practice and seeing how much tedious work goes on behind the scenes is both daunting and thrilling. Design feels like a world of endless exploration, and coding is an endless rabbit hole for me to dive into. In design and writing, it is important to create a hook, or something that gives the audience a reason to lean in closer. I tried to do that while obscuring the actual content of the text, through the h2 tab. I tried to create a silly scatter poem from the text to change the meaning of what is being conveyed. This project definitely gave me a deeper understanding of how a designer can use visual clues to extract a certain narrative from the content we're working with. This is an immensley important skill, especially when working with clients, ensuring you can communicate what they are trying to get across visually.
