MID-CENTURY MODERN

02/14/2024

a visual guide to mid-century modern style

This assignment was certainly a challenge. At first I thought the orange in the screenshot was a part of the webpage and I tried to include the thick orange border in my webpage. This was incredibly tricky. I added it in the * and the body section, and each try produced a failure. I was able to figure out more about how the cascading works in CSS. The fact that the text below affects the text above has been a difficult concept for me to understand, but this exercise helped a lot. However, when it came to the footer, no matter what I did, I couldn’t change the color of the text without messing up another text element. The top part of the webpage was successful, in my opinion. I feel like my spacing was as close to the reference as I could get it. The trick was recognizing when to go back into HTML and add a < br >. If I had more time I would get my webpage to a replica; however, after over an hour I had to peel myself away and accept defeat. It was difficult to get the list at the bottom aligned, but once I did, the process felt pretty intuitive in retrospect.
